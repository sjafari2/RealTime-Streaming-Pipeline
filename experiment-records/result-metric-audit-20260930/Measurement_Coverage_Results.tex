\subsection{Measurement coverage and interpretation}\label{sec:result-metric-coverage}\label{app:four-condition-diagnostics}
Each run is reported separately. Mean completion latency, p99, unfinished percentages and one-second SLO violations use messages produced during evaluation and successfully accepted by Kafka. Each message is counted once and followed through the end of drain. Completion is measured when processing finishes, before offset-commit acknowledgment. Warm-up messages are excluded from these outcomes but can still contribute to backlog and throughput.

Lag, throughput, CPU and memory plots cover evaluation. Resource cost and CPU and memory use accumulated over time cover consumers during evaluation and drain. Missing or invalid observations, including those during partition handover, remain gaps rather than being treated as zero.

Lag skew and persistent hot partitions are interpreted alongside total backlog and growth: a high relative skew does not necessarily mean a large backlog. The runtime-state plots describe conditions before and after scheduled mitigation. The metric definitions are given in the Metrics section; Table~\ref{tab:result-metric-coverage} in Appendix~\ref{app:measurement-coverage} lists where their results are reported.
